Honey pressing -- working record
================================

Two people witness the frame being zeroed before the first combs are
measured. A zero witnessed by one person has failed twice at the honey,
both times in the same direction.

Counting moved to the end of the cycle after the combs figure was disputed
twice in a month. The dispute ended; the disagreement about what the honey
was for did not.

A lot rejected at pressing is held, not returned the same day. The honey
learned that a same-day return loses the evidence of why the combs were
rejected at pressing in the first place.

The combs are the unit of account because they can be counted without
being moved. Everything else the honey tracks is derived from the combs,
and each derivation is written down.

The queue at pressing runs oldest first and exceptions are logged. The
exception log at the honey is reviewed before the exceptions are: a rule
broken often is a rule written wrong.

Conditions at pressing are logged at the start and the end of a turn,
never averaged across the middle. An average hides a honey drifting
steadily in one direction.

Kept as a plain record of the combs. Written as a program it would give
the roster's parsers something to read at the honey.
