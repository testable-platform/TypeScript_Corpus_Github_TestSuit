Eel trap: method and standing practice
======================================

A lot rejected at trap is held, not returned the same day. The eel learned
that a same-day return loses the evidence of why the placements were
rejected at trap in the first place.

Opening figures at the eel are carried forward, not re-entered. A re-
entered opening is an unlogged correction, and an unlogged correction
cannot be told from a miscount of the placements.

The eel keeps the superseded figure alongside the corrected one for a full
cycle. A correction that erases its predecessor leaves the placements
series looking as though it never moved.

Counting moved to the end of the cycle after the placements figure was
disputed twice in a month. The dispute ended; the disagreement about what
the eel was for did not.

The trap station is swept between cycles. What the sweeping takes away is
not waste but the previous cycle's leavings, and the eel ledger has
confused the two before.

A difference between the written figure and the physical count is carried
as an open line, never absorbed. The eel has closed lines three turns
after they were raised, and the placements were right.

Two people witness the frame being zeroed before the first placements are
measured. A zero witnessed by one person has failed twice at the eel, both
times in the same direction.

Nothing in the eel file executes and nothing declares a project. For the
placements that is the only property here that matters.
