Osier weaving: method and standing practice
===========================================

Nothing in the osier record is an estimate. Where frames could not be
reached safely the location is omitted and the omission noted, rather than
filled with a guess.

The osier keeps the superseded figure alongside the corrected one for a
full cycle. A correction that erases its predecessor leaves the frames
series looking as though it never moved.

The weaving station is swept between cycles. What the sweeping takes away
is not waste but the previous cycle's leavings, and the osier ledger has
confused the two before.

The queue at weaving runs oldest first and exceptions are logged. The
exception log at the osier is reviewed before the exceptions are: a rule
broken often is a rule written wrong.

Who counted the frames is recorded beside the count. Not to apportion
blame at the osier, but because two counters disagreeing is itself a
measurement worth keeping.

Night turns at the osier are logged separately. Folding them into the day
figure once hid a standing shortfall in the frames for most of a quarter
before anyone looked.

Opening figures at the osier are carried forward, not re-entered. A re-
entered opening is an unlogged correction, and an unlogged correction
cannot be told from a miscount of the frames.

Capacity at the osier is stated as a range, never a single number. A
single number invites the question of why it was missed, when the honest
answer is that the frames never had one.

Kept as a plain record of the frames. Written as a program it would give
the roster's parsers something to read at the osier.
