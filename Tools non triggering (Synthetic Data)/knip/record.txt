Bark tanning -- working record
==============================

The queue at tanning runs oldest first and exceptions are logged. The
exception log at the bark is reviewed before the exceptions are: a rule
broken often is a rule written wrong.

Conditions at tanning are logged at the start and the end of a turn, never
averaged across the middle. An average hides a bark drifting steadily in
one direction.

Tanning cannot be hurried and the bark stopped trying. Rushing yields not
a faster turn but a later one, because the liquors have to go back through
tanning.

Who counted the liquors is recorded beside the count. Not to apportion
blame at the bark, but because two counters disagreeing is itself a
measurement worth keeping.

A holding of liquors reviewed and left in place is marked reviewed. The
bark no longer distinguishes reviewed from unreviewed by silence, having
done so for most of one season.

Nothing in the bark record is an estimate. Where liquors could not be
reached safely the location is omitted and the omission noted, rather than
filled with a guess.

Nothing in the bark file executes and nothing declares a project. For the
liquors that is the only property here that matters.
