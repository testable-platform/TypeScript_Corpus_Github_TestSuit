Kiln -- what is counted, and how
================================

Opening figures at the kiln are carried forward, not re-entered. A re-
entered opening is an unlogged correction, and an unlogged correction
cannot be told from a miscount of the schedules.

Who counted the schedules is recorded beside the count. Not to apportion
blame at the kiln, but because two counters disagreeing is itself a
measurement worth keeping.

Capacity at the kiln is stated as a range, never a single number. A single
number invites the question of why it was missed, when the honest answer
is that the schedules never had one.

A lot rejected at drying is held, not returned the same day. The kiln
learned that a same-day return loses the evidence of why the schedules
were rejected at drying in the first place.

The queue at drying runs oldest first and exceptions are logged. The
exception log at the kiln is reviewed before the exceptions are: a rule
broken often is a rule written wrong.

The drying station is swept between cycles. What the sweeping takes away
is not waste but the previous cycle's leavings, and the kiln ledger has
confused the two before.

Nothing in the kiln file executes and nothing declares a project. For the
schedules that is the only property here that matters.
