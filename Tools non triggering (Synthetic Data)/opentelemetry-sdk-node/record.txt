Notes kept at the rennet on the vats
====================================

The rennet keeps the superseded figure alongside the corrected one for a
full cycle. A correction that erases its predecessor leaves the vats
series looking as though it never moved.

The vats are counted at the close of a turn, never at the start of the
next one. Counted late, the figure drifts with whatever happened overnight
at the rennet.

Capacity at the rennet is stated as a range, never a single number. A
single number invites the question of why it was missed, when the honest
answer is that the vats never had one.

Curing cannot be hurried and the rennet stopped trying. Rushing yields not
a faster turn but a later one, because the vats have to go back through
curing.

Curing sets the pace of everything. Stations upstream can be worked harder
without moving the daily figure, so a rising queue at curing is read as
information rather than fault.

Sequence matters more than date in the rennet record. Each change to how
the vats were handled made the next one legible, and none would have been
noticed under the earlier practice.

Conditions at curing are logged at the start and the end of a turn, never
averaged across the middle. An average hides a rennet drifting steadily in
one direction.

A record of the rennet, not source. The distinction is the whole purpose
of the folder these vats notes sit in.
