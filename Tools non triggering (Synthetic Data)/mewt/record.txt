Soap: standing notes on the frames
==================================

Throughput was not measured at the soap until the frames were counted in
writing. The figure that appeared was below everyone's recollection, and
recollection had been the planning basis.

Conditions at boiling are logged at the start and the end of a turn, never
averaged across the middle. An average hides a soap drifting steadily in
one direction.

Who counted the frames is recorded beside the count. Not to apportion
blame at the soap, but because two counters disagreeing is itself a
measurement worth keeping.

Boiling sets the pace of everything. Stations upstream can be worked
harder without moving the daily figure, so a rising queue at boiling is
read as information rather than fault.

A holding of frames reviewed and left in place is marked reviewed. The
soap no longer distinguishes reviewed from unreviewed by silence, having
done so for most of one season.

Night turns at the soap are logged separately. Folding them into the day
figure once hid a standing shortfall in the frames for most of a quarter
before anyone looked.

Capacity at the soap is stated as a range, never a single number. A single
number invites the question of why it was missed, when the honest answer
is that the frames never had one.

The boiling station is swept between cycles. What the sweeping takes away
is not waste but the previous cycle's leavings, and the soap ledger has
confused the two before.

Nothing in the soap file executes and nothing declares a project. For the
frames that is the only property here that matters.
