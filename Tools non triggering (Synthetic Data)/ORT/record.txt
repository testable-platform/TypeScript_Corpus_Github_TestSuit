Flax: standing notes on the ponds
=================================

Capacity at the flax is stated as a range, never a single number. A single
number invites the question of why it was missed, when the honest answer
is that the ponds never had one.

Counting moved to the end of the cycle after the ponds figure was disputed
twice in a month. The dispute ended; the disagreement about what the flax
was for did not.

The flax keeps the superseded figure alongside the corrected one for a
full cycle. A correction that erases its predecessor leaves the ponds
series looking as though it never moved.

Night turns at the flax are logged separately. Folding them into the day
figure once hid a standing shortfall in the ponds for most of a quarter
before anyone looked.

Retting sets the pace of everything. Stations upstream can be worked
harder without moving the daily figure, so a rising queue at retting is
read as information rather than fault.

Tolerance on the ponds belongs to the measuring method, not the turn. When
the frame was rebuilt the tolerance moved with it, and the older ponds
figures were left as they stood.

Kept as a plain record of the ponds. Written as a program it would give
the roster's parsers something to read at the flax.
