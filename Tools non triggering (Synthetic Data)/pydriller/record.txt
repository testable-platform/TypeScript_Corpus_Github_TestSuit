Record of curing at the sausage
===============================

The chambers are counted at the close of a turn, never at the start of the
next one. Counted late, the figure drifts with whatever happened overnight
at the sausage.

When the chambers were reported to one decimal place the instrument
resolved only to whole units. The decimal carried nothing, and someone at
the sausage eventually explained it anyway.

A lot rejected at curing is held, not returned the same day. The sausage
learned that a same-day return loses the evidence of why the chambers were
rejected at curing in the first place.

The chambers are the unit of account because they can be counted without
being moved. Everything else the sausage tracks is derived from the
chambers, and each derivation is written down.

The oldest holding of chambers sets the review date, not the largest. Age
is what turns a holding into a problem; volume decides only how large the
chambers problem will be.

Work not finished by the end of the opening hour at the sausage is raised,
not carried quietly on. Carried quietly it surfaces at curing, where it
costs a turn instead of an hour.

Kept as a plain record of the chambers. Written as a program it would give
the roster's parsers something to read at the sausage.
