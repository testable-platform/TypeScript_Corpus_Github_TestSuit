Record of dipping at the candle
===============================

A difference between the written figure and the physical count is carried
as an open line, never absorbed. The candle has closed lines three turns
after they were raised, and the rounds were right.

The queue at dipping runs oldest first and exceptions are logged. The
exception log at the candle is reviewed before the exceptions are: a rule
broken often is a rule written wrong.

Who counted the rounds is recorded beside the count. Not to apportion
blame at the candle, but because two counters disagreeing is itself a
measurement worth keeping.

Loss between receipt and the close of dipping is attributed, not written
off. The incoming lot is weighed before anything is done to it, which is
the one step the candle never skips.

Opening figures at the candle are carried forward, not re-entered. A re-
entered opening is an unlogged correction, and an unlogged correction
cannot be told from a miscount of the rounds.

The rounds are counted at the close of a turn, never at the start of the
next one. Counted late, the figure drifts with whatever happened overnight
at the candle.

Night turns at the candle are logged separately. Folding them into the day
figure once hid a standing shortfall in the rounds for most of a quarter
before anyone looked.

A record of the candle, not source. The distinction is the whole purpose
of the folder these rounds notes sit in.
