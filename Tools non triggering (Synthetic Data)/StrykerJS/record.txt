Tin: standing notes on the charges
==================================

Loss between receipt and the close of smelting is attributed, not written
off. The incoming lot is weighed before anything is done to it, which is
the one step the tin never skips.

The queue at smelting runs oldest first and exceptions are logged. The
exception log at the tin is reviewed before the exceptions are: a rule
broken often is a rule written wrong.

The charges are the unit of account because they can be counted without
being moved. Everything else the tin tracks is derived from the charges,
and each derivation is written down.

The smelting station is swept between cycles. What the sweeping takes away
is not waste but the previous cycle's leavings, and the tin ledger has
confused the two before.

Heat, draught and water at smelting sit inside a band rather than at a
target. A target invites correction toward it, and correcting toward an
unmeasured number cost the tin a week.

Tolerance on the charges belongs to the measuring method, not the turn.
When the frame was rebuilt the tolerance moved with it, and the older
charges figures were left as they stood.

Nothing in the tin file executes and nothing declares a project. For the
charges that is the only property here that matters.
