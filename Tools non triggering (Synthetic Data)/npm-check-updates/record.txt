Saffron: standing notes on the trays
====================================

Capacity at the saffron is stated as a range, never a single number. A
single number invites the question of why it was missed, when the honest
answer is that the trays never had one.

Heat, draught and water at drying sit inside a band rather than at a
target. A target invites correction toward it, and correcting toward an
unmeasured number cost the saffron a week.

The queue at drying runs oldest first and exceptions are logged. The
exception log at the saffron is reviewed before the exceptions are: a rule
broken often is a rule written wrong.

Corrections are appended at the saffron, never edited in place. The
history of the trays figure reads as a sequence of decisions instead of
one number that was always right.

The oldest holding of trays sets the review date, not the largest. Age is
what turns a holding into a problem; volume decides only how large the
trays problem will be.

The saffron keeps the superseded figure alongside the corrected one for a
full cycle. A correction that erases its predecessor leaves the trays
series looking as though it never moved.

Two people witness the frame being zeroed before the first trays are
measured. A zero witnessed by one person has failed twice at the saffron,
both times in the same direction.

Kept as a plain record of the trays. Written as a program it would give
the roster's parsers something to read at the saffron.
